IT Acceptable Use Policy

Purpose
This policy defines acceptable use of organizational computing devices, networks, and
accounts.

General Principles
Organizational systems are provided to support assigned job duties. Incidental
personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work performance, consume
significant resources, or violate any other organizational policy or law.

Prohibited Activities
Users must not install unlicensed software, attempt to bypass security controls,
share login credentials with another person, or use organizational systems to access,
store, or transmit unlawful content. Connecting personal storage devices to systems
handling sensitive data is prohibited unless the device is organization-encrypted.

Email and Communication
Email accounts are organizational property and may be monitored for security and
compliance purposes. Employees should exercise caution with unsolicited attachments
and links and report suspected phishing attempts to IT Security immediately.

Software and Device Management
All laptops and mobile devices used to access organizational data must have endpoint
protection software active and must be enrolled in the device management platform.
Operating system and security patches must be applied within the timeframe specified
by IT Security, typically 14 days of release.

Enforcement
Violations of this policy may result in suspension of system access and, depending on
severity, disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or
contract.
