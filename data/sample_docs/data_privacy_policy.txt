Data Privacy Policy

Purpose
This policy sets out how personal data collected from employees, customers, and
members of the public is handled, stored, and protected.

Scope
This policy applies to all personal data processed by the organization, whether held
digitally or on paper, and to all staff, contractors, and third-party vendors who
handle that data on the organization's behalf.

Collection and Use
Personal data is collected only for specified, legitimate purposes disclosed to the
individual at the time of collection. Data must not be used for a new purpose
incompatible with the original purpose without fresh notice or consent.

Data Minimization and Retention
Only the minimum data necessary for the stated purpose should be collected. Records
containing personal data are retained according to the organization's retention
schedule and securely destroyed once the applicable retention period expires.

Access and Correction Requests
Individuals may request a copy of the personal data held about them, or request
correction of inaccurate data, by submitting a request to the Privacy Office. Requests
are acknowledged within 5 business days and completed within 30 calendar days.

Breach Notification
Any suspected or confirmed data breach involving personal data must be reported to the
Privacy Office immediately upon discovery. Depending on severity, affected individuals
and relevant regulators may need to be notified within statutory timeframes.

Third-Party Vendors
Vendors who process personal data on the organization's behalf must be bound by a data
processing agreement specifying security obligations and breach notification duties.
