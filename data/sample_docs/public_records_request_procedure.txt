Public Records Request Procedure

Purpose
This procedure explains how members of the public may request access to non-exempt
records held by the organization.

Submitting a Request
Requests must be submitted in writing through the public records portal or by mail to
the Records Office, and should describe the records sought with reasonable
specificity, including any relevant date range.

Response Timeline
The Records Office will acknowledge a request within 5 business days and provide a
substantive response, including responsive records or a written explanation of any
withheld material, within 20 business days. Complex requests may be extended by an
additional 10 business days with written notice to the requester.

Exemptions
Certain categories of records are exempt from disclosure, including records containing
personal identifying information of private individuals, active law-enforcement
investigation files, and documents protected by attorney-client privilege. Exempt
portions of an otherwise responsive record are redacted rather than withholding the
entire document where feasible.

Fees
The first hour of staff time and the first 100 pages of copies are provided free of
charge. Additional staff time is billed at the published hourly rate, and additional
pages are billed at the published per-page rate, with an estimate provided before work
proceeds if fees are expected to exceed $50.

Appeals
A requester who disputes a denial or redaction may appeal in writing to the Records
Office director within 30 calendar days of the response. The director's decision on
appeal is the organization's final administrative determination.
