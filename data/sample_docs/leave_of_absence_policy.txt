Leave of Absence Policy

Purpose
This policy describes the types of leave available to employees and the process for
requesting extended time away from work.

Types of Leave
Covered categories include medical leave, parental leave, bereavement leave, jury duty,
and personal leave of absence. Each category has its own eligibility rules and maximum
duration, summarized in the leave schedule maintained by Human Resources.

Medical Leave
Employees may take up to 12 weeks of job-protected medical leave per rolling 12-month
period for a serious health condition, supported by appropriate medical certification.
Intermittent leave may be approved for ongoing treatment schedules.

Parental Leave
Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of parental leave following the birth,
adoption, or foster placement of a child. The first 2 weeks may be paid at full salary
under the organization's supplemental parental leave benefit.

Requesting Leave
Leave requests should be submitted to Human Resources at least 30 days in advance where
foreseeable, or as soon as practicable for unforeseeable circumstances such as a medical
emergency. Required documentation varies by leave type.

Return to Work
Employees returning from leave are reinstated to the same or an equivalent position,
unless the leave period or applicable law provides otherwise. A return-to-work
conversation with the manager and Human Resources is scheduled prior to the employee's
first day back.
