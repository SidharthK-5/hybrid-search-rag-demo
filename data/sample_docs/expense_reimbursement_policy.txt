Expense Reimbursement Policy

Purpose
This policy explains which business expenses are reimbursable and how to submit a
claim.

Eligible Expenses
Reasonable costs for travel, lodging, client meals, conference registration, and
mileage incurred in the direct performance of assigned duties are eligible. Mileage is
reimbursed at the standard published rate, recalculated each fiscal year.

Submission Process
Claims must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the expense date,
with an itemized receipt attached for any single expense over $25. Claims submitted
after 60 days will not be processed except with written approval from a department
head.

Approval Thresholds
Expenses under $500 require manager approval only. Expenses between $500 and $5,000
require both manager and finance business-partner approval. Expenses above $5,000
require director-level sign-off before the trip or purchase is committed, not after.

Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol outside of client entertainment, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and
upgrades to premium travel class without prior written approval are not reimbursable.

Corporate Card Use
Employees issued a corporate card must reconcile all transactions monthly. Repeated
late reconciliation may result in suspension of card privileges.
