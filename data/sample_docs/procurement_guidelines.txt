Procurement Guidelines

Purpose
These guidelines govern how the organization purchases goods and services from
external vendors, to ensure fair competition and responsible use of funds.

Purchase Thresholds
Purchases under $2,500 may be made directly by a department using an approved
purchase order. Purchases between $2,500 and $25,000 require at least two competing
quotes. Purchases above $25,000 require a formal request for proposal (RFP) process
managed by the Procurement Office.

Vendor Qualification
New vendors must complete a vendor registration form and pass a basic financial and
compliance screening before any purchase order is issued in their name. Preferred
vendors are reviewed annually and may be removed from the preferred list for repeated
quality or delivery issues.

Conflicts of Interest
Staff involved in selecting a vendor must disclose any personal or financial
relationship with that vendor before participating in the decision. Failure to
disclose a conflict of interest may result in disciplinary action and voiding of the
resulting contract.

Contract Terms
All contracts above $25,000 must include defined deliverables, a payment schedule tied
to milestones, and a termination-for-convenience clause. Legal review is required
before signature for any contract exceeding one year in duration.

Emergency Procurement
In situations posing an immediate risk to safety or operations, a department head may
authorize an emergency purchase outside the standard thresholds, with a retroactive
justification filed with the Procurement Office within 5 business days.
