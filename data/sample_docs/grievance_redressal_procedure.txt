Grievance Redressal Procedure

Purpose
This procedure gives employees a clear path to raise concerns about workplace
treatment, policy application, or working conditions, and to have those concerns
addressed fairly.

Informal Resolution
Employees are encouraged, where comfortable and appropriate, to first raise a concern
directly with their manager or the other party involved. Many concerns can be resolved
informally at this stage.

Formal Grievance Submission
If informal resolution is not appropriate or does not resolve the concern, the employee
may submit a formal written grievance to Human Resources describing the issue, relevant
dates, and any desired outcome. Formal grievances are logged and assigned a case
reference number.

Investigation
Human Resources will acknowledge receipt within 3 business days and complete an
investigation, including interviews with relevant parties, within 20 business days.
Timelines may be extended for complex cases, with the employee notified of the revised
timeline and reason for delay.

Outcome and Appeal
The employee receives a written outcome summary at the close of the investigation. If
the employee disagrees with the outcome, they may request a review by a more senior
Human Resources leader within 10 business days of receiving the outcome.

Protection from Retaliation
Retaliation against an employee for raising a grievance in good faith is strictly
prohibited and is itself grounds for disciplinary action against the retaliating
party.
