Remote Work Policy

Purpose
This policy defines the conditions under which employees may perform their duties
from a location other than their assigned office.

Eligibility
Employees who have completed at least 90 days of continuous employment and whose
role does not require regular physical presence (such as facilities, security, or
front-desk staff) may request a remote work arrangement. Approval is granted by the
employee's direct manager in consultation with Human Resources.

Work Hours and Availability
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during their team's agreed core hours,
typically 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, and to attend scheduled meetings with video
enabled unless a documented accommodation applies.

Equipment and Expenses
The organization provides a laptop and standard peripherals for remote work. Employees
may submit a one-time home-office stipend request of up to $300 for desk, chair, or
monitor purchases, subject to manager approval and retention of receipts.

Data Security
Remote employees must connect to internal systems only through the approved VPN client
and must not store confidential documents on personal, unencrypted devices. Any lost or
stolen device used for work purposes must be reported to IT Security within 24 hours.

Review and Revocation
Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually. The organization reserves the right to
revoke a remote arrangement if performance expectations are not met or if business needs
change, with 30 days' written notice where practicable.
